[Kelpwatt](https://kelpwatt.angelov.uk) is a web app, so there's nothing to download from Google Play or the App Store. You can still install it: your phone or computer adds a Kelpwatt icon, and it opens in its own window, without the browser's address bar.

Installing it is worth doing on a phone:

- Kelpwatt is one tap away on your home screen.
- [Notifications](https://kelpwatt.angelov.uk/blog/notifications) show with Kelpwatt's name and icon. On an iPhone or iPad, they only work in the installed app.
- It's always up to date. There are no app store updates to wait for.

## Android

1. Open [kelpwatt.angelov.uk](https://kelpwatt.angelov.uk) in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu at the top right.
3. Tap **Install app**. If you see **Add to home screen** instead, tap it, then choose **Install**, not **Create shortcut**. A shortcut only opens a browser tab.
4. Kelpwatt appears on your home screen and in your app list. Open it from there.

The app shares Chrome's login, so you're usually still logged in.

Other browsers can install it too: in **Samsung Internet**, tap the menu, then **Add page to → Home screen**. In **Firefox**, tap **⋮**, then **Add to Home screen**.

## iPhone and iPad

1. Open [kelpwatt.angelov.uk](https://kelpwatt.angelov.uk) in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up). On an iPhone it's at the bottom of the screen, or behind the **⋯** button in newer versions of iOS.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**. If you see **Open as Web App**, leave it on.
4. Tap **Add**. Kelpwatt appears on your home screen.
5. Open Kelpwatt from the home screen. It takes Safari's login with it, so you're usually still logged in. If not, log in.

From then on, the app and Safari have separate logins: logging out in one doesn't log you out of the other.

Chrome and Edge on iPhone can do the same: tap **Share** in the address bar, then **Add to Home Screen**.

Notifications need iOS 16.4 or later. To check, go to **Settings → General → About**.

## Windows, Mac and Linux

In **Chrome** or **Edge**, open [kelpwatt.angelov.uk](https://kelpwatt.angelov.uk) and click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (a monitor with a down arrow). If it isn't there:

- **Chrome:** **⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install page as app**.
- **Edge:** **⋯ → Apps → Install this site as an app**.

Kelpwatt then opens in its own window, and you can pin it to your taskbar or dock.

On a Mac, Safari can do the same from **File → Add to Dock**.

## Turn on notifications

Once Kelpwatt is installed, open it, go to **Settings → Notifications** and turn on **Notifications on this device**. [Get a notification when your Growatt battery needs you](https://kelpwatt.angelov.uk/blog/notifications) explains what you'll be notified about and how to choose.

## Removing it

Remove it like any other app: long-press the icon and choose **Uninstall** on Android or **Remove App** on iPhone. On a computer, open Kelpwatt, then use the **⋮** or **⋯** menu in its window.

Removing the app doesn't delete your Kelpwatt account. Before you remove it, turn off **Notifications on this device** in Settings, or log out.
